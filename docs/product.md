# Product decisions

These are settled. Change them only when Emmett says so.

## Who gets in

- **The Roster.** Olympians, pros and top D1 athletes. They're invited directly and get approved fast after a roster check. They're the reason everyone else wants in.
- **Everyone else applies.** College athletes, surfers, skaters, fitness people, coaches. They can pick more than one category. The application asks for six photos, a profile song, an optional member vouch and one short question.

## The wait

- After applying, people see only "Received." No place in line, no timeline, no status updates.
- Rejected applicants are never told. They just keep waiting.
- Accepted applicants get an email: "You're in." The acceptance never expires. They open the app whenever they want.

## Money

- Nobody uses the app for free. It's $7.99 a month.
- Two ways to pay: a card (Apple Pay) or a code. Codes come only from the founder, for people he personally comps.
- No free preview. Nothing inside the app is visible until membership starts.

## Inside the app

- **Discover** has two modes: Date and Friends.
- **Messages.**
- **You:** hide from my school, pause my profile, vouches left (2), membership status.

## Growth

- Word of mouth only. No ads and no press.
- The website is one line.
- Seed it first with Olympians and USC athletes.

## Privacy

- Nobody should look like they're on a dating app. No public profiles, no visible join dates, no member numbers, no "founding" badges.
- Screenshots of profiles get a warning, then removal.

## Ruled out

- 48-hour deadlines on acceptance.
- A free trial before the paywall.
- Waitlist positions or numbers.
- Founding member numbers or status badges.
- A public network or careers tab, for now.
