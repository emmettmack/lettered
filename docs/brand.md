# Brand

## Feel

A private room, not a dating app. Quiet and confident. The less it explains, the more people want in.

## Look

- Monochrome: night `#0C0C0B`, night-2 `#161614`, night-3 `#24231F`, bone `#F1EEE7`, ash `#8C8980`, smoke `#5C5A54`.
- Type: Newsreader (light weight) for the wordmark and headlines, Hanken Grotesk for everything else.
- The wordmark is lowercase: `lettered`.
- Photography carries the color. The interface stays out of the way.
- One primary button per screen, in bone on night.

## Voice

- Short declarative sentences. Say less.
- Speak to people who already think they're the best. Never beg, never sell.
- No exclamation points, no emoji, no hype words.
- Don't explain the committee or the process. Mystery is the point.

## Approved lines

- Website: "A private room for athletes."
- Roster invite: "You don't need to apply."
- After applying: "Received."
- Acceptance email: "You're in. Your acceptance doesn't expire."
- Accepted screen: "The committee said yes. It doesn't say that often."
- Roster accepted: "You were expected."

## Avoid

- Copying Raya's screens, names or wordmark.
- "Olympic" or school logos in branding.
- Anything that sounds like a hookup app.
